I read Gals! and fell in love with it

I tend to stick with stuff I'm comfortable with. It's one of my weaknesses as an otaku. I try to branch out and read unfamiliar works, but I tend to gravitate towards my mainstays. So when someone recently reached out to me for shoujo manga recommendations I was scandalized. I didn't have any! Shoujo manga is HUGE and as an otaku, to have such little experience with shoujo manga is a disgrace... I'm nothing more than a waterflea posing as a human...

This meant it was time for me to put my money where my mouth is. If I want to act like some elitist know-it-all otaku I better start studying, and where better to start than with Shueisha's Monthly Ribon Magazine? I looked into what they've serialized and found Gals!. It was the perfect match for an otaku like me.

Live life like a fire! Kotobuki Ran is a fierce and independent girl! She doesn't sit idly by in the face of injustice! She asserts her own worldview onto reality to create the Shibuya she wants to live in! She'll help a girl in need, or take out the trash! Kotobuki Ran gets it done and she does it with style! She's strong and independent but still feminine. But instead of societies definition of femininity she represents her own form of femininity. That's what being a gal is all about, right?

The manga gives a strong feeling of authenticity. While I don't know what it meant to be a Shibuya gal in the late nineties, Mihono-sensei was immersed in the culture as a gal herself and even provides tips within the manga on things like how to decorate your nails, or how to coordinate an outfit. It gave me the feeling that, despite the exaggerations of the manga, Gals! is a realistic look at what it meant to be a Shibuya gal.

I really loved the depiction of the girls' friendship. They're three different people, with their own circumstances. They butt heads and haven't always seen eye to eye, but their friendship is based in mutual understanding and respect. They're united by their rejection of societies expectations, and by their desire to be their own autentic selves, unshackled!

The manga has a bunch of additional content inside it like diaries by Mihono-sensei, "Dissection of a Bloodbath" - which details the manga production process between Mihono-sensei and her mother (who helped with the manga! Cute!), and "My Big Bro's Little Room" which features bizarre art drawn by her big brother! This sort of thing seems common for Ribon?

Gals! won me over thanks to its exciting characters and heartwarming story. I want to live in a world with more people like Kotobuki Ran who stand up for what they believe in and won't stay silent in the face of injustice. Gals! provides a world where you can be true to yourself, free from judgment

Gals! won me over because of its characters, who fight against society to be who they want to be, and its story which seriously tackles issues that young girls faced at the time. It's a realistic manga with colourful characters!

Going forward, I'm gonna try harder and harder to read manga I normally wouldn't think to. Immersing yourself in other worlds with an open mind and a pure heart is an ironclad rule for otaku!
