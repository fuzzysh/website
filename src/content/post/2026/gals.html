I read Gals! and fell in love with it

I tend to stick with stuff I'm comfortable with. It's one of my weaknesses as an otaku. I try to branch out and read unfamiliar works, but I tend to gravitate towards my mainstays. So when someone recently reached out to me for shoujo manga recommendations I was scandalized. I didn't have any! Shoujo manga is HUGE and as an otaku, to not have any experience with shoujo, is a disgrace... I'm nothing more than a waterflea posing as a human...

This meant it was time for me to put my money where my mouth is. If I want to act like some haughty know-it-all otaku I better start studying, and where better to start than with Shueisha's Monthly Ribon Magazine? I looked into what they've serialzed and found Gals!. It was the perfect match for an otaku like me.
